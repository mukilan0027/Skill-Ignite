DIAGNOSTIC SCENARIO

Based on the active schematic telemetry, the vehicle is experiencing an 85% sudden drop in motor torque. The VCU reports the DC Bus is stable at 800V. Which component is the root cause of the failure?

A
HV Battery Pack - Internal cell matrix thermal runaway causing voltage sag.
B
Traction Inverter - Phase B desynchronization failing to convert DC to 3-phase AC properly.
C
Vehicle Control Unit (VCU) - Lost CAN communication with the motor resolver.
D
AC Motor - Stator winding short to ground causing total mechanical bind.
Correct Diagnosis!
The DC Bus line is flowing normally, isolating the battery. The red indicators on the Inverter and the broken Phase B AC line confirm an inversion failure, causing the torque drop at the motor.
Incorrect Analysis
Look closely at the energy flow paths in the schematic. The DC Bus line is operating nominally, but there is an error pulse inside the conversion block.
